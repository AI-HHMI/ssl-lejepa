e00/metric-examples report

e00/metric-examples: instance-segmentation metrics on a real crop with one kind of mistake added at a time. The crop, its eight corrupted labellings and their scores (PQ, SQ, RQ, VOI split and merge, adapted Rand error, and boundary AP of the hard labelling) come from the top-level metric_examples.py, run on the cluster; this builds the page. Which mistake costs which metric?

15objects in the crop

Findings

What each metric penalizes, in this crop

  • PQ and RQ count objects; VOI and Rand error count voxels. 300 specks cost PQ 0.999 to 0.091 (RQ 0.091: each speck is a false object) and VOI 0.02, Rand error 0.001. Deleting the smaller half of the objects costs PQ 0.636 (RQ) and Rand error 0.001. This is why the size filter decides PQ in mws.toml while VOI barely moves. SQ is 0.999 and 1.0 in both: the matched objects are exact.
  • One big merge or split is a small mistake to PQ and a large one to VOI. Cutting the largest object in two: PQ 0.955 (one object of 15 is wrong), VOI split 0.27, Rand error 0.095. Merging it with its neighbour: PQ 0.935, VOI merge 0.66, Rand error 0.299. VOI names the direction (split or merge, never both here); PQ and Rand error do not. Rand error and VOI weight a mistake by the voxels it touches.
  • Shattering is the worst for everything: PQ 0.082 (RQ 0.096: no piece overlaps its object by IoU above 0.5; SQ 0.849 for the few that do), VOI split 4.37, Rand error 0.917.
  • Boundary placement shows in SQ and in short-range AP, little elsewhere. Every object shifted 2 voxels: Rand error 0.056 and VOI 0.34 / 0.25, but PQ 0.692 (SQ 0.798, and RQ 0.867 as small objects drop under IoU 0.5) and short-range AP 0.046 (the +1 pairs are all at the boundaries) against long-range AP 0.655. A 1-voxel gap between objects: PQ 0.709, short AP 0.213, long AP 0.734. Long AP is the more tolerant: its +10 pairs straddle boundaries by a margin.
  • AP here is of hard labellings (the probe's soft predictions would score differently). Specks barely move it (short 0.933, long 0.986); deleting objects costs short AP 0.466 against long 0.934 (an erased voxel has no same-object pairs at +1, and few at +10).

Caveats. One crop, a few large objects: a 1 µm³ crop has 15 objects, so one mistake is a large share of its PQ; the eight mistakes are synthetic, a real segmentation makes several at once; the crop is of the hemibrain EB test block, which the probe is scored on.

Examples

exact: The ground truth itself: every metric at its best. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 64

shifted: Every object moved 2 voxels in x: all boundaries in the right place up to 2 voxels, no object wrong. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 64

eroded: A 1-voxel gap (label 0) between touching objects, as thresholding or watershed leave: objects slightly small. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 64

split: The largest object cut in two by an x plane: one split. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 63

merge: The largest object merged with the neighbour it touches most: one merge. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 63

shattered: Every object cut into pieces by a 32-voxel grid: many splits, each piece a plausible object. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 64

dust: 300 small (2^3) fragments of new ids inside objects: a speck is a false object. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 73

missed small: The smaller half of the objects (by count) deleted: tiny share of voxels, half of the objects. Columns: EM | truth | labelling | boundaries (green both, red missed, blue extra); z 64
