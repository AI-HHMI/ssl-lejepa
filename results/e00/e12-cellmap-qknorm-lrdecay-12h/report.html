Findings

What e12 shows so far

Only three of the five runs have trained, and the probe says the changes did not help it. d3 (masked on lmd4_pow04, no CellMap) and d4 (masked on CellMap alone) have waited in the queue since submission and have not started, so the CellMap comparison is not made. d1 finished its 62.5k steps (5.7 h on 8 B300s, 16M samples); d0 (259k of 325k steps, 58M samples) and d2 (87k of 100k, 20M) are at 11.2 h of their 12 h and will stop before the lr reaches 0 (now 2.0e-5 and 1.3e-5): they ran 0.149 and 0.443 s a step against the 0.127 and 0.41 of e11.

  • Training is stable. No skipped step; after step 10k the gradient norm stays under 2.1 (MAE), 1.0 (masked) and 0.35 (views), against e11's spikes to 72-300 late. The MAE's resid_norm falls from 32 to 19-35 (e11: 51 to 236), the masked run's levels off at 60-100 (e11: 125 to 314), views' wanders at 166-348 (e11: 157 to 278). qk_norm and the lr decay did not cost anything, but the 1M-step instabilities of e11 are beyond what these runs have reached (259k), so they are not shown to be fixed.
  • MAE probes as e11's did: 0.293 short / 0.865 long at 80k, 0.292 / 0.867 at 160k (e11: 0.302 / 0.878 at 80k, 0.297 / 0.876 at 160k). e11's decline began after 160k, so there is nothing to compare yet: the 320k milestone does not exist, and the run's end (step 259k) is not probed.
  • Masked 96 is worse than e11's masked 64 at every milestone: short AP 0.151 at 20k and 0.128 at the end (e11: 0.172 at 20k, 0.177 at 40k), long AP 0.752 at 40k (e11: 0.841). It peaks at 20k and falls although the lr decays, so the fall is not the constant lr. It differs from e11's in three ways: a bigger view (12^3 tokens against 8^3), a bigger masked cube (mask_block 0.6 of 12 is 7^3 tokens, 343 predicted, against e11's 5^3, 125) and a quarter hemibrain_wide, the probe's own family. Confounded: it does not say which of the three costs the probe. d5 (d1 with a 5^3 cube) is added to separate the mask's size; it has not run.
  • Views at 80k steps: 0.180 short, 0.667 long, against e11's 0.213 and 0.721. Its long AP is still rising at 80k.
  • Validation loss (32 fixed windows a family; the held-out blocks against training volumes). MAE: no gap on hemibrain_wide (0.510 against 0.525 at 160k), microns (0.417 both) or CellMap (0.632 against 0.600); on liconn_mouse the gap widens from 0.07 (5k) to 0.15 (0.578 against 0.432, 160k) while both fall. Masked: gaps of 0.04 (hemibrain_wide), 0.11 (fish2), 0.09 (microns), none for CellMap or liconn_mouse at the end. Views: the held-out loss rises while the training loss falls on three families: hemibrain_wide 0.181 to 0.310 (training 0.138 to 0.101), microns 0.223 to 0.428 (0.207 to 0.231), liconn_mouse 0.214 to 0.273 (0.168 to 0.140): the invariance objective does overfit its training volumes, the one run with a clear overfitting signal. The probe's fall in e11 was not shown to be this: the MAE and masked losses fall on held-out blocks while the probe falls.

Caveats. Single seeds. The held-out blocks are single volumes, each a different distribution from its family's training volumes, so a gap is partly distribution shift; CellMap's own are labelled crops inside the training volumes' footprint. The probe is the unstandardized, 3000-step fit of e11 (Eval.standardize off), so the milestone numbers compare with e11's and not with a converged probe. d0 and d2 stop before lr 0 and have no end evals yet.

Next. Run d3 and d4 (the data comparison), re-pull when d0 and d2 finish, and refit the probes with Eval.standardize on the kept milestones to separate the probe's fall from the encoder's.
