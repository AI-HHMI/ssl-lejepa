Models

MAE: encode a quarter of the tokens, decode the rest to voxels

flowchart LR
    I["view<br/>Batch 1 Z Y X"] --> T["patchify<br/>tokens"]
    T -- "25% visible" --> E["ViT encoder<br/>12 x 512"]
    T -- "75% hidden" --> Q["mask tokens<br/>+ position"]
    E --> D["decoder<br/>2 x 192"]
    Q --> D
    D --> P["predicted voxels<br/>of hidden patches"]
    T -. "normalised target" .-> L(["MSE loss"])
    P --> L

Masked tokens: predict a hidden cube of a shifted view

flowchart LR
    R["region<br/>view + shift"] --> C["context view"]
    R --> G["target view<br/>offset by < 24 vox"]
    C -- "visible tokens<br/>cube removed" --> E["ViT encoder<br/>12 x 512"]
    G -- "all tokens" --> E2["same encoder<br/>(shared weights)"]
    E --> PR["predictor<br/>4 blocks<br/>+ mask tokens"]
    PR --> PJ["projector<br/>MLP"]
    E2 --> PJ2["projector<br/>MLP"]
    PJ --> INV(["prediction error<br/>masked tokens"])
    PJ2 --> INV
    PJ --> SG(["SIGReg"])
    PJ2 --> SG
